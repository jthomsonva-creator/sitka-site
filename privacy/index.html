Privacy Policy

Last updated 26 November 2026

Sitka is a guitar tuner and songwriting app. It has no accounts, no analytics and no servers. This policy is short because there is very little to describe.

What Sitka collects

Nothing. Sitka has no account system and asks for no personal information. We run no servers, so there is nowhere for your data to go and nothing of yours for us to hold. We cannot see your songs, your tunings or your practice history, because none of it leaves your phone.

The only thing Sitka talks to is Google Play or the App Store, about the one-time unlock and the occasional rating prompt. None of it carries anything of yours. See Network access below.

The microphone

Sitka needs microphone access to measure the pitch of your guitar or your voice. That is the only reason it asks for it.

Audio is analysed as it arrives and thrown away, never recorded or stored. Sitka does not record audio, does not save audio to a file, and does not transmit audio anywhere. What survives a moment of listening is a pitch measurement — a number — and nothing else.

Plug-in mode and the practice amp process audio entirely on your device. Nothing is recorded or sent.

Sitka listens only while a screen that needs the microphone is open. There is no background listening and no foreground service. Leave that screen and the microphone is released.

What is stored on your phone

Sitka saves the following in its own private storage on your device:

  • songs and ideas, including lyrics, chords, capo, key, tempo and section structure
  • your custom tunings (up to 20), which tuning is selected, and the ones you used recently
  • My shapes, the chord shapes you save
  • the Explore stack, the shapes you are working with
  • your chord practice setup
  • your vocal range, and your vocal practice sessions: pitch scores and the dates you practised
  • settings, such as reference pitch, the chords capo, metronome and Perform preferences
  • whether you own the unlock, as Google Play or the App Store last reported it, so the app can open without a network
  • for the rating prompt: how many days you have used the app, and when it last asked

This lives in the app's private area, readable only by Sitka. It is not uploaded anywhere, and it is not included in your phone's automatic cloud backup — not Android's, and not iCloud's on an iPhone. Sitka keeps it out deliberately, so your lyrics are never copied somewhere you did not choose. Uninstalling Sitka deletes all of it.

Backing up your work

Because nothing is uploaded, a backup is something you make on purpose. A backup file is made only when you tap Export: Setup → Back up & restore writes one file holding your songs, tunings, My shapes, practice history and settings, and hands it to your phone's share sheet — mail it to yourself, or keep it wherever you keep things. The same screen restores from that file. It is plain readable text, so you can open it and see exactly what is in it.

That file is the only copy of your work outside the app, and it goes wherever you send it. It never contains the unlock.

Sharing a song

Sitka can hand a song to your phone's share sheet, so you can mail it to yourself or save it wherever you keep things. When you do that you choose the destination and the file goes there directly. Nothing is sent to us, and Sitka is not involved after the hand-off.

Network access

Sitka has no servers, no account, no analytics and no ads. The only thing it talks to is Google Play or the App Store, in three ways:

  • when the app opens, it asks the store whether you own the one-time unlock;
  • it fetches the price when you look at the unlock, and the store handles the purchase if you make one;
  • the store shows the occasional ‘rate Sitka’ prompt, and Setup’s “Rate Sitka” opens Sitka’s page in the store.

Each of those is between your device and Google or Apple. There is no Sitka server involved, because we do not run one.

Nothing else leaves your device. Your songs, lyrics, tunings, shapes, vocal range and practice history are never transmitted — not to us, not to Google or Apple, not to anyone. There is no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising and no account.

Children

Sitka is not directed at children and collects no information from anyone, of any age.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.
